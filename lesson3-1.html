📘 What Is Digestion?

Breaking down food with teeth and changing it into a form that is easy to absorb is called digestion.

🍎 ➡️ 🦷 ➡️ 🍽️
🤖

Brainy says: Digestion helps our bodies process the food we eat. The journey begins in the mouth!

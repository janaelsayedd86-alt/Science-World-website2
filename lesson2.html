  • Compare how different gases affect burning.

🌍 What Is Air Made Of?

Air is a mixture of different gases, such as nitrogen, oxygen, and carbon dioxide.

🔬 What Did We Discover?

Let's compare the effect of different gases on burning.

Gas Observation Result
Oxygen 🫧 Burns brightly 🔥 Supports burning
Nitrogen 💨 Flame goes out Does not support burning
Carbon Dioxide ☁️ Flame goes out Does not support burning

⭐ Brainy's Conclusion: Oxygen is the gas that helps things burn!

⭐ Let's Remember!

  • Air contains about 78% nitrogen.
  • Air contains about 21% oxygen.
  • Oxygen helps things burn.
  • Nitrogen and carbon dioxide do not support burning.

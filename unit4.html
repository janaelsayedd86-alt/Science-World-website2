  • Identify the heart as the organ that pumps blood.
  • Understand what blood carries throughout the body.
  • Learn how blood circulates through blood vessels.
  • Understand why the pulse becomes faster after exercise.
  • Discover how the kidneys filter waste products and excess water.
  • Learn how urine is produced, stored, and discharged.
  • Identify the locations of important organs in the body.
  • Understand how body organs are connected by blood vessels.

1. Air Passages and Burning

For things to keep burning, fresh air must constantly flow in and out.

In a place where the air is not replaced, things cannot keep burning.

2. What Happens to a Candle?

When the candle is not covered with a lid:

Fresh air is supplied from the surroundings, so the candle keeps burning.

When the candle is covered with a lid:

Once the air inside the jar is used up, the flame goes out.

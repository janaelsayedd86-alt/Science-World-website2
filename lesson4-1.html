❤️ The Heart and Blood

❤️

The Heart

The heart works like a pump. It pumps blood out to the whole body and receives blood returning to it.

🩸

The Blood

Blood carries oxygen and nutrients to the whole body, and collects carbon dioxide and waste produced in the body.

🛣️

Blood Vessels

Blood flows through blood vessels that spread all over the body.

💓 What Is a Heartbeat?

❤️

Heartbeat

The movement of the heart during pumping out blood is called a heartbeat.

Pulse

The heartbeat travels through the blood vessels and can be felt as a pulse at the wrist.

🏃 Let's Measure Your Pulse!

⏱️

Pulse Measurement Activity

1. Put your finger near the base of your thumb on your wrist and find where it is beating.
2. Count how many times your pulse beats in one minute.
3. After jumping lightly, measure it again.

Click Start and count your pulse beats for 15 seconds. Then multiply your count by 4 to estimate the number of beats in one minute.

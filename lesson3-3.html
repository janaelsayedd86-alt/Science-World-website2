🍎 What Is Absorption?

After food is digested, it becomes small nutrients. These nutrients need to enter the body to be used.

Absorption:
The nutrients from digested food are mainly absorbed in the small intestine along with water.

After that, more water is absorbed in the large intestine. What is left is eliminated from the anus as feces.

🌀 Discover the Villi!

The inside wall of the small intestine has small bumps called villi.

Villi:
Small bumps on the inside of the small intestine. They increase the inner surface area, helping the intestine absorb nutrients and water efficiently.

How Do Villi Look?

The villi are close together, creating a larger surface area for absorbing nutrients and water.

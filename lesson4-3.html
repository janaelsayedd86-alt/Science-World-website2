📍 Organ Locations

🫁

Chest

The left and right lungs are located in the chest. The heart is between them. The ribs protect the heart and lungs.

🍽️

Abdomen

The stomach is at the top of the abdomen. The small intestine is below it, and the large intestine surrounds it. The liver is also in the upper abdomen.

🫘

Lower Back

There are two kidneys at the back, one on each side of the backbone.

🩸 How Are Organs Connected?

Blood vessels: Tubes that carry blood through the body and connect its organs.

Our organs do not work separately. They are connected by blood vessels. Blood carries oxygen and nutrients to the organs and collects waste products from the body.

🫁 Lungs

Provide oxygen to the blood.

🫀 Heart

Pumps blood throughout the body.

🩸 Blood Vessels

Carry oxygen and nutrients to organs.

🍎 Digestive Organs

Digest food and absorb nutrients.

🫘 Kidneys

Filter waste products and excess water.

Remember: The blood circulation system connects the organs and helps the whole body work together.

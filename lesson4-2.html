🫘 What Are the Kidneys?

Kidneys: Two organs that filter waste products and excess water from the blood.

Our body produces waste products. These unnecessary substances are carried by the blood to the kidneys.

We have two kidneys. They are located in the lower back, one on each side of the backbone.

Educational illustration showing the two kidneys on both sides of the backbone.

⚙️ How Do the Kidneys Work?

Blood carries waste products from different parts of the body to the kidneys. The kidneys filter out waste products and excess water. These substances become urine.

🩸 Blood enters the kidneys
🫘 Waste products and excess water are filtered out
💧 Urine is produced
🫙 Urine is stored in the bladder
🚻 Urine leaves the body

📖 How Do Our Lungs Work?

Our lungs are two important organs inside the chest. They help our bodies get oxygen from the air and remove carbon dioxide.

🫁 The Right and Left Lungs

We have two lungs. They are protected by the rib cage inside our chest.

🌬️ The Diaphragm

The diaphragm is a dome-shaped muscle below the lungs. It helps us breathe by moving down and up.

🩸 The Blood

Blood carries oxygen from the lungs to different parts of the body. It also carries carbon dioxide back to the lungs.

🔍 Activity 1: Explore the Respiratory System

Click on each part to discover its function.

The nose is the main entrance for air. It helps filter, warm and moisten the air.
The trachea is a tube that carries air from the throat toward the bronchi.
The bronchi are two main branches that carry air into the lungs.
Alveoli are tiny air sacs. Oxygen passes from the alveoli into the blood, while carbon dioxide passes from the blood into the alveoli.
The diaphragm is a muscle below the lungs. Its movement helps air enter and leave the lungs.

🫧 What Happens Inside the Alveoli?

Alveoli are tiny air sacs surrounded by small blood vessels. They are the main places where gas exchange takes place.

🫧 Alveoli: Oxygen enters the blood.
⬇️ OXYGEN (O₂)
🩸 Blood: Receives oxygen.
⬆️ CARBON DIOXIDE (CO₂)
💨 Alveoli: Receive carbon dioxide to be exhaled.

1. Oxygen enters the blood

Oxygen moves from the air inside the alveoli into the surrounding blood vessels.

2. Carbon dioxide leaves the blood

Carbon dioxide moves from the blood into the alveoli.

3. Carbon dioxide is exhaled

When we breathe out, carbon dioxide leaves our body.

🫁 Activity 3: How Do the Lungs Move?

Watch how the lungs and diaphragm move during breathing.

🫁

Diaphragm: Relaxed

Choose an action to see how breathing works.

During Inhalation

The diaphragm contracts and moves downward. The chest cavity becomes larger, allowing air to enter the lungs.

During Exhalation

The diaphragm relaxes and moves upward. The chest cavity becomes smaller, helping air leave the lungs.

🩸 The Role of Blood

Blood is very important in respiration. It transports gases between the lungs and body cells.

❤️ Carrying Oxygen

Blood carries oxygen from the lungs to different parts of the body.

💨 Carrying Carbon Dioxide

Blood carries carbon dioxide from body cells back to the lungs to be removed.

📘 Part 1: Air Passages and Burning

For things to keep burning, fresh air must constantly flow in and out.

🕯️ What happens to a candle inside a jar?

Without a lid: Fresh air comes from the surroundings, so the candle keeps burning.

With a lid: When the air inside the jar is used up, the flame goes out.

📗 Part 2: The Role of Oxygen

Air is a mixture of gases, including nitrogen, oxygen, and carbon dioxide.

🌬️ Which gas helps things burn?

🟢 Oxygen helps things burn.

🔵 Nitrogen does not help things burn.

🔵 Carbon dioxide does not help things burn.

Oxygen is the gas in air that helps things burn.

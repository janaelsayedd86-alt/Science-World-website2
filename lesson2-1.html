📖 What Is Respiration?

Respiration is an important life process. Our bodies need oxygen to release energy from food.

🌬️ Inhalation

Inhalation is the process of taking air into the lungs. The inhaled air contains oxygen.

💨 Exhalation

Exhalation is the process of pushing air out of the lungs. Exhaled air contains more carbon dioxide than inhaled air.

🫁 The Respiratory System

The respiratory system helps us breathe. Air passes through different parts until it reaches the lungs.

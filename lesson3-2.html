💧 What Is the Function of Saliva?

When we eat food like rice or bread, it contains a substance called starch.

Saliva in the mouth helps break down starch and changes it into another substance that is easier for the body to digest.

Remember:
A liquid that has the function of digesting food, like saliva, is called a Digestive Juice.

Digestive juices are produced in:

👄

Mouth

Saliva helps break down starch.

🫃

Stomach

Digestive juices are also produced here.

🌀

Small Intestine

Digestive juices are also produced here.

👄 Mouth: Saliva starts the digestion of starch found in foods such as rice and bread.
🫃 Stomach: The stomach also produces digestive juices.
🌀 Small Intestine: The small intestine also produces digestive juices.

🔬 Experiment Steps

1. Put starch solution in both test tubes.
2. Add water to tube A.
3. Add saliva to tube B.
4. Place both tubes in hot water at about 40°C for a few minutes.
5. Add 1 or 2 drops of dilute iodine solution.
